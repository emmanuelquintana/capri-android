# Changelog (English)

The same as CHANGELOG.md (Spanish, the original), in English: the app shows it when it's in English.

## 1.11.0

- **Chat is the heart of the app:** opening Capri now takes you straight to her chat, to talk with her comfortably,
  with your whole history (what you write in her bubble too). She answers with her same memory, mood and powers. If
  she was asleep, she wakes up on her own. What you can ask her is behind the “?” button.
- **Games get their own tab** in the pill menu, each game one tap away.
- **3 new games:** Connect 4, Memory (with her faces) and Neural sequence. In the first two she plays against you
  and remembers, like in the others.
- **Floating Capri steps aside while you play** in the app, so she doesn’t cover the game (optional, in Games). She
  still watches from the corner.
- **Her old animations are back,** including eating instant noodles. The ones redrawn in 1.10.0 are gone (yes, the
  six seven one too). Only the one lying on the keyboard stays, now calmer.
- **Sleep mode:** Capri sleeps with you. In You › Sleep mode (or “Capri, sleep mode, wake me at 7”) she curls up on
  your screen, counts how many times you turn it on during the night and scolds you when you do. With an alarm, she
  wakes you and doesn’t stop until you turn it off (with “5 more minutes”). In the morning she tells you how long you slept.
- **Reaction mode:** tell her “Capri, watch videos with me” and, while you watch a video, she watches with
  you, gives her opinion now and then and notes it in her chat to remember it. “Turn off reaction mode” stops it. She needs
  to be able to see your screen.
- **Braindances:** give her lots of love (pet her, talk to her) and every so often she records you a braindance: a
  memory of hers, scene by scene, to watch in the app (You › Braindances). Switch to the thermal layer to find hidden
  details. At the end she tells you what she thinks, and you tell her what you thought. Also: “Capri, give me a
  braindance”.
- **She talks in the singular:** no more “we” or “we love”. She talks about herself as one person.
- **DONATE button** under Start Capri, Discord and TikTok: opens the window to support her on PayPal.
- **Her pill in the status bar (Android 16+):** next to the clock, with her mood, and in Samsung’s Now Bar, with
  buttons to chat, open her menu or hide her. Depends on the phone; it can be turned off in Settings.
- **Better on Huawei and Honor:** in Settings, a card to let her run in the background (otherwise the phone closes
  her), lift her battery restriction and install a voice. Permissions open the right screen on those phones, and
  store links go to AppGallery when there’s no Play Store.
- **New Discord link.**
- The Community tab moved into What’s new, and Commands behind the chat’s “?”.
- **Capri Voice (her recorded voice):** Capri can now speak in her own voice, phrase by phrase and offline, in Latin
  American Spanish and English: when you pet her, throw her, come back, say goodbye or go to sleep, with the exact
  words she says. Her AI's answers are said as written (phone voice or text only, your choice) and are never swapped
  for another phrase. Set it up in Customize › Capri Voice. Her voice packs are optional and will arrive separately.

## 1.10.1

- **The second Capri no longer spins:** when you split her and music plays, her twin kept turning around as if it
  couldn’t decide where to go. Now it dances and turns on the beat, once in a while.
- **Microphone on Huawei (and others):** Capri said she had no microphone permission even when she did. The one
  missing it was the phone’s speech app. Now she tries another recognizer and, if none can, tells you which app
  needs the permission.
- **Spotify plays the song:** if you chose Spotify, “play this song” now plays it, not just searches for it.
- **Installs on Huawei and Honor:** the APK now also carries the classic signature some installers (Huawei’s among
  them) ask for. If it still won’t install, the website explains what to turn off (“Pure mode”).
- **Support Capri (optional):** once in a while, never in the first days, a note shows up to support her on PayPal.
  Tap “Don’t show again” and it never comes back.
- **Cleaner animations:** in several of them (dancing, guitar, laughing, stretching, listening, some game ones…)
  Capri slid sideways from one frame to the next. Now she stays in place. Where her mane was cut in a straight
  line, it now fades out softly.

## 1.10.0

- **Her new look:** all her animations, poses and faces are redrawn with her real mask (the green plate with her
  big eyes), plus two new ones: lying on your keyboard and on her phone while you play.
- **No more missing arm:** in light mode, on the wardrobe's big picture and in its thumbnails she was
  drawn with one arm. Now she has both hands in every outfit, in any mode.
- **The chat no longer moves while you type:** the keyboard changes size a bit as it suggests words, and that kept
  moving her chat.
- **Banks:** many banking apps won't work with another app on top. If you gave her "Usage access", Capri hides by
  herself while you use your bank and comes back when you leave.
- **Your songs on Spotify (optional):** in "Customize", choose whether the songs you ask for play on YouTube or
  Spotify.
- **No more blank answers:** if the AI answers nothing, Capri asks again or uses another model.
- **She talks in the singular:** she hardly says "we" anymore. In English she no longer mixes in Spanish.
- **Microphone:** on phones that listen a few seconds at a time, it turns on and off less in silence.
- **Looking at your screen with Groq:** if Groq has no model that sees, she uses another of your AIs instead of
  asking you to switch.
- **Goodbye white screen while dancing:** when Capri danced, listened to music or rested, on many phones she turned
  into a white block, then the whole screen went white and the app closed. Her animations now use far less graphics
  memory, and if your phone still goes down twice during one, she stays still (no animations) for a few days instead.
- **Play with her:** chess, minesweeper, 21, tic-tac-toe, ping pong and rock-paper-scissors, no internet needed. She plays against you and says what
  she thinks. In the «You» tab, or tell her "Capri, let's play".
- **Her memory no longer vanishes:** if Capri saved something before she finished reading what she remembered about
  you, she wiped it all. Now she waits to read it first, and keeps a backup copy.
- **Out of nowhere:** her Quick Settings tile or her notification button makes her appear with a glitch and a hello,
  even while you watch videos or play. If you turn on "She uses your phone for you", Android's accessibility button
  does it too.
- **Cyberpunk games:** the games screen is now Night City neon, with Capri watching you from the corner, her mask as
  her piece in tic-tac-toe, on her cards and on her ping pong paddle, and a stamp at the end of each game.
- **Capri plays like a real rival:** she no longer answers instantly or always the same way. She has a mood (gets
  cocky, annoyed, in the zone) and it shows on her face and in how she plays. She thinks longer when the move is hard,
  sometimes eyes one move and changes her mind, makes human mistakes (and sometimes notices), comments on your good
  moves and your slips, hurries you if you take long and gets serious if you keep beating her. She remembers your
  habits between games (your chess opening, when you stand at 21, your favourite hand) and keeps the score against
  you. At rock-paper-scissors she sometimes tells you what she'll throw… and it may be a lie. At ping pong she reacts
  with a delay, gets tired on long rallies and smashes when she's confident. At minesweeper she gives you a hint if
  you get stuck.
- **Two Capris:** spread two fingers on her (or tell her "Capri, split") and there are two. The other one walks,
  dances to your music and talks to you if you tap her. Tap her twice or drag her onto Capri to join them.
- **She uses your phone for you (optional):** "Capri, go back", "go home", "open notifications", "scroll down",
  "tap Settings" or "type here hello". Turned on in Accessibility («Customize» tells you how). She only acts when you
  ask, never in banking or payment apps, and never types passwords.
- **Chat history:** in «You», everything you told her and what she answered, by day, with search. It stays on your
  phone only, and you can delete it.
- **Your question isn't cancelled anymore:** if you tap outside her chat while she's thinking, she keeps thinking.
  When the answer is ready she shows it in her bubble, with a button to keep talking.
- **Messages by WhatsApp:** "Capri, tell Ana on WhatsApp I'm on my way". She opens WhatsApp (or Telegram, Messenger,
  SMS…) with the message written; you pick who and send it.
- **Her beam:** when you uninstall an app, Capri shoots it and it disintegrates.
- **She dresses herself (optional):** in «Customize», every 6 h, 12 h or a day she picks another outfit and shows it
  off. Her colors never change.
- **Battery saver (optional):** in «You», pick 15, 20 or 30%. At that level and not charging, what uses the most
  (her ears, her live look, her animations) rests until you charge her.
- **Icon mode with more faces:** she laughs, winks, gets surprised, angry, in love, thinks with little dots, sways
  to your music, gets dizzy if you drag her a lot and falls asleep if you leave her alone.
- **She remembers you better:** she keeps more about you, learns your phrases and uses them now and then, and every
  so often asks how you are.
- **While you type:** she lies on top of the keyboard; in games, she's on her phone.
- **Her voice can be turned off:** in «Customize › Her voice». She still listens and answers in writing.
- **Microphone:** fixed "error 12" on some Samsung phones with Android 16.
- **Looking at your screen:** if your AI can't see pictures, she uses another of yours that can (Gemini, Groq…).
- **Groq:** no longer picks a voice model by mistake that asked to accept terms.
- **Notifications and music bar:** on some Xiaomi phones they didn't show until a heavy game was opened. They now
  reconnect by themselves.

## 1.9.1

- **Fewer closes and white screens:** on phones with weak graphics (Redmi, Honor, realme…) Capri closed or stayed
  white. In **lite mode** she's now drawn without 3D, and if your phone loses her picture twice in a row, it
  switches to that drawing by itself for a few days.
- **OpenRouter:** looking at photos and videos no longer fails with "something went wrong". If you run out of
  credits, she tells you clearly.
- **Gamer mode with cloud games:** detects Boosteroid, GeForce NOW, Xbox Cloud, Steam Link, Moonlight, Parsec and
  others.
- **She peeks into your notifications:** she no longer shows up every time or gets stuck; she peeks once and rests
  a while.
- **Microphone:** no more repeated "The speech recognizer failed (error 5)", she remembers how your phone listens, and no
  more beep each time the mic turns on and off (media sound is muted only for an instant; never your calls, alarms
  or notifications).
- **With a mouse:** if you use your phone with a mouse, you can now click Capri.

## 1.9.0

- **Report a bug:** in Settings, tell us what happened and it goes straight to Emma on Discord, with the version and
  Capri's internal error log (without your name, your messages or your keys).
- **Her menu sideways:** with the phone on its side (while gaming, say) it fits in two columns and has a bar that
  shows there's more below.
- **Wardrobe:** the Sandevistan aura no longer looks cut up (now it's three clean trails), the See-through options
  show you how she'll look, and on some phones her preview no longer shows up as a white box.
- **Your classes and your calendar:** in the «You» tab, note which days you have class and at what time, and an hour
  before her special notification arrives, with her face and her colors. If you want, she also reads your calendar
  (read only, she never changes anything) and reminds you of what's coming: the evening before and an hour before.
  If she's on your screen, she tells you herself too. Nothing leaves your phone.
- **Her icon in your notifications:** the bar at the top now shows her little face instead of a white square, on her
  quick settings tile too.
- **Share things with her:** in any app, *Share → Show to Capri* with a picture, a video or a text. She opens her
  chat and tells you what she thinks, like a friend you sent it to; then you keep talking about it. From a video
  she sees three moments. Her AI has to see pictures.
- **She translates your screen:** "Capri, translate this" (or "…into Spanish", "…into Japanese") in her chat or out
  loud: she looks at your screen and writes the message, post or page in the language you ask for, or in yours.
- **Tips for your games:** "Capri, give me a tip" or "what do I do here?": she looks at your game and tells you
  something useful for that moment. In gamer mode, with her live look on, she gives you one every few minutes (off in
  Settings).
- **Streaming mode:** when you're live (TikTok, Twitch, YouTube, Kick…), she reads your viewers' comments on your
  screen and answers them out loud, as your co-host. No accounts, no connection to those apps. In Settings or
  "Capri, streaming mode".
- **Her accent:** in Settings › Talk to her, pick how she sounds in your language: Mexico, Spain, Argentina,
  Colombia…; American or British; Brazil or Portugal; France or Québec.
- **She reacts on her own to what you're doing:** with music or a video playing she listens along, dances or plays
  air guitar; with TikTok or YouTube open she snacks while you watch; while you play she lies down to watch you.
  Music needs no permission anymore; to know which app is open she uses "Usage access" if you already gave it.
- **You can tell she's listening:** when you say "Capri" and talk to her, little bars show up by her head, moving
  with your voice.
- **Discord at hand:** the Discord button is now right under the one that starts her.
- **No more getting stuck on start:** on some phones her picture got lost, leaving a grey box with a sad face. Now
  she recovers on her own, and if the phone can't draw her the usual way, she's drawn simpler instead of not at all.
- **Fewer "servers are swamped":** with the free AI she waits for her turn instead of giving up, and if yours is
  full another one you gave her a key for answers, so she always replies.
- **Your birthday:** now and then she asks when it is (or tell her: "my birthday is May 3rd"), and on the day she
  celebrates you: confetti, she wishes you a happy birthday out loud and throws her party.
- **Cyberpsychosis effect:** in her Wardrobe › Effects, red copies of her twitching around her, like the chrome
  taking over.
- **Her voice no longer wakes up when you move the phone:** with the strict filter she ignores what she hears while
  the phone is moving (its rustle, not you), and her name alone has to be heard clearly.
- **If her page freezes** (a half-drawn panel, nothing moving), Android gives her a fresh one and she's back.
- **Faster answers:** she keeps the connection to her AI open between messages and gets it ready when you open her
  chat, so your message goes out without waiting to connect; and if a server gets stuck she tries another model sooner.
- **Switching tabs no longer stutters:** the app no longer rereads all its settings on every switch, and the
  transition is shorter and smoother.
- **Light mode for older phones:** in Settings › Performance. It turns on by itself on phones with little memory or an
  old Android: she's drawn with fewer pixels and her menus lose the blur, so she runs smoother. And if the phone's
  "Android System WebView" is too old for her, the app tells you and takes you to update it.
- **Fewer grey boxes:** her window uses much less graphics memory, which is what left parts of her grey and made her
  close and reopen by herself.
- **The mic no longer turns on and off nonstop:** on Android 13 or newer she listens continuously (the mic stays on,
  steady) instead of starting over every few seconds; on others she rests a little when there's nothing to hear.
- **Finer "Strictest" filter:** she ignores far-away voices, like the TV or someone across the room.
- **Settings sorted into three tabs:** **You** (what she calls you, your birthday, what she remembers about you and
  how she looks after you), **Customize** (her wardrobe, how she looks and behaves on your screen, and her voice) and
  **Settings** (the general: permissions, language, her AI, her ears, her modes and performance). Permissions no
  longer have their own tab: they're at the top of Settings.
- **Your birthday in the app:** in the You tab, pick the date without waiting for her to ask.
- **New animations:** she listens with a hand to her ear, waves in streaming mode, blows out her cake's candles and
  throws her party on your birthday.
- **Anonymous statistics:** once a day the app says "someone uses Capri" with a random number (not your phone's), the
  version, the language and the Android version, to know how many people use her. Nothing personal. Turn it off in
  Settings.
- **Ghost mode just for her:** your touches go through Capri, but her notification and music cards can still be
  touched. To let touches through them too, turn on Customize › "Her cards too".
- **She peeks into your notifications (optional):** when you pull down the notification panel or quick settings,
  Capri peeks in from the side and waves. Turn it on in Customize with the Accessibility permission (off by default);
  she's only told the panel opened, she reads and touches nothing. Note: some banks won't open with Accessibility on.
- **Capri widget:** put it on your home screen, in her colors and with her face (it changes when she's off or put
  away): start her, talk, have her look at your screen, dress her, and put her away or bring her out, in one tap.
- **She hears you when you pause a video:** she used to take up to 3 seconds to listen again after you paused
  YouTube and missed the start of what you said; now she starts the moment the sound stops.

## 1.7.0

- **Icon mode:** only her green mask stays on screen, eyes open, and she keeps commenting from there (what she sees
  on your screen, cheers in gamer mode, her chat, a line now and then). Drag it with your finger wherever it bothers
  you least; tap it to open her menu. So you can keep her big and, whenever you want, have her take up almost
  nothing. Turn it on in *Settings*, in her menu or by saying “Capri, icon mode” (and “Capri, icon mode off”).
- **No gravity:** she stays right where you put her, even in the air. She doesn’t wander off on her own, doesn’t jump
  onto your notifications, and if you fling her she doesn’t fly away, so she doesn’t get mad anymore. Great for
  leaving her in a corner or next to your chats. In *Settings* or her menu; she remembers where you left her.
- **She remembers you:** after a chat, Capri notes down what matters about you (what you like, the people you tell
  her about, what’s going on with you, how things went) and remembers it next time, so you pick up where you left
  off instead of starting from scratch. See it and delete it in *Settings → What she remembers about you* (one thing
  or everything), and turn it off there too. It stays only on your phone; to note it down she asks her AI once per
  chat, once you’re no longer talking with her (with the free AI she waits her turn so you don’t have to wait).
- **NVIDIA AI:** you can now give her a free NVIDIA key (build.nvidia.com), with lots of models to pick from. She
  picks a fast one to chat and, to see your screen, an NVIDIA one that can see pictures. If a model is gone, she
  moves on to the next one by herself.
- **More languages:** in *Settings → Idioma · Language* pick Automatic, Español, English, Français, Português or
  Русский, for the app and for what she says. Voice commands (“Capri, …”) work in those languages too.
- **New Community tab** in the app’s bottom bar: the Discord and the TikTok are there now (they used to be at the
  end of *Settings*), and that’s where new community things will go.
- **She comments on what you watch:** with “Watch my screen live” on, when you switch videos on TikTok, Reels or
  YouTube she gives her opinion on what she sees (at most once every 50 seconds).
- **She can see your screen with more AIs:** if your key is from a service whose chat model can’t see pictures (Groq,
  for example), she looks with one from the same service that can.
- **She no longer vanishes when a notification arrives:** she jumps to where the card ends up, not where its
  animation starts.
- **The keyboard no longer closes on its own** while you type to her: she doesn’t move while her chat is open.
- **She connects on older phones:** on some with Android 8 (like the Sony Xperia XA1) setting up her AI failed with
  “Can't create handler inside thread that has not called Looper.prepare()”. Not anymore.
- **Errors you can understand:** instead of technical messages like “messages content must be a string” or “signal
  lost”, she tells you in plain words what happened.
- **Voice chat:** if the AI takes a while to answer, she says out loud that she’s thinking, instead of going silent.

  open a game and off when you leave (if you turn it off mid-game, she respects that). It needs Android’s “Usage
  access” permission, which only shows which app is open. It’s off by default: some banking and antivirus apps
  distrust that permission.

- **Music bar your way:** in *Settings → Music bar* choose *Always* (stays while music plays), *A while* (as before)

- **Notices higher up:** your notification cards no longer show up mid-screen, but at the top, right where Capri

## 1.5.0

- **Capri no longer shoots off:** when you tapped her or let go after dragging, she sometimes flew off as if you’d
  thrown her. Now she’s only thrown if you really fling her with a quick move; if you just let go, she drops softly.
  And when you throw her, she goes with less force.
- **Pick her size:** in *Settings → Capri on your screen*, from mini to huge.
- **Ghost mode:** Capri stays on your screen but can’t be touched and doesn’t get in the way: your taps go through to
  the app below, like a ghost shimeji. Turn it on in *Settings* or her menu.
- **More movement:** she no longer stands still. Choose how restless she is in *Settings → Movement*: calm (as
  before), normal or restless. She walks, jumps, spins, stretches and looks around on her own.
- **Capri sees your screen:** tell her “Capri, look at my screen” (or tap *Look at my screen now* in Settings, or in
  her menu) and she comments on what she sees. With *Watch live* she keeps looking and reacts on her own now and
  then, or when you switch apps, until you turn it off. Android asks you each time she starts looking, and you’ll see
  the cast icon while she does. Screenshots aren’t saved: they only go to her AI. Protected apps (banks, passwords)
  look black to her. It needs an AI that sees pictures, like Gemini (its key is free); the free AI can’t see, and
  Capri tells you so.
- **The chat no longer freezes:** we found out why it was slow: the free AI only allows one answer every 45 seconds
  per phone, and if you wrote before that, it waited or failed. Now Capri waits her turn and asks again on her own,
  asks the AI to think less so it answers faster, and meanwhile tells you she’s thinking instead of going silent. If
  you want instant answers, give her a free Gemini key.
- **She picks a better model on her own:** she prefers fast models that really are chat models, and remembers the
  list for a few minutes so she doesn’t ask every time.
- **Your AI key no longer gets erased:** sometimes, if the AI was slow or failed once, Capri dropped the key and said
  she had no AI. Now she only removes it if it’s really invalid.
- **She talks more like Capri:** in the chat she answers like someone you’re texting, not like an assistant: she
  reacts, jokes, gives opinions and answers what you actually said. In Spanish she talks like a Latina, with some
  Mexican slang.
